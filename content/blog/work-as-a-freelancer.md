### Responsibility

To be work as a freelancer, you have to so much under discipline. At the top of these, time planning and motivation.

**What Else ;**

-   It will be necessary to start this work alone as a hero,
-   You will find yourself in a new agreement in every new project,
-   You should find your business by yourself and sometimes it will be really difficult at the beginning.
-   If you were working from an office, it will be difficult at the beginning for you to work by yourself without your teammate. (Maybe to be work at some coffee shop,co-works spaces, etc…)
-   You will pay your medical insurance, income tax or other taxes, etc …
-   If you can’t plan your time good, you can mess up life with your work,
-   7/24 you will manage all emailing, phone traffic, etc.. by yourself.
-   You will plan all of the project’s process from the beginning until publishing by yourself.

Some of the base responsibilities are above which I list. But probably you will face more stuff than I said.

### What Should I Pay Attention To In Some Freelance job websites?

![](/blog/work-as-a-freelancer/image-4.jpeg)

I would like to give you a few tips on this subject, and you should carefully examine the profiles of the project providers in the websites I shared in the previous topic. What are these;

-   **Project descriptions,**
-   **Comments,**
-   **Defined payment methods (such as credit card),**
-   **Payment Type (Fixed Price, hourly)**
-   **Payment facilities for your country,**
-   **Taxation**

**The Pricing**

On such websites, usually, two payments are made. This type of payment depends on the determination of the project owners or your bidding by the project. Also, the relevant site receives its own share from the project at a certain rate. In order to turn this situation into your own, keep your relationship with the project owners so that you can get a job by removing the relevant websites.

**Hourly Wages**

Hourly wages are calculated on the basis of the minimum hourly working wage set by **Freelancer**. This situation may be different when the project comes to you and it may be different when you bid for a project.

**Fixed Price**

Fixed pricing is generally based on the freelancer’s bidding on a project with a certain fee. The price set by the project owner usually indicates the maximum price he can give or can be considered as the fixed price that the freelancer will set.

### How should the project be planned and what should I pay attention to?

There is no reason not to take the project if you do not have any previous experience and believe that you can handle the project when buying a freelance project.

-   If there is no project analysis presented to you before you start the project, you need to analyze it and prepare it and report it to the relevant project owner. But the main thing is to remember that this is part of the work. can offer work with project analysis. In this case, feel free to charge for the analysis.
-   You need to be careful with fixed price projects, or it may cause you trouble. Because you usually receive your money at the end of the job. When the work is finished, there is always a master. You need to plan your time very well.
-   In freelance projects, you should divide the work into milestones and define a fee for each milestone. This is the biggest factor that will finance you until the end of the project. In addition, it will visualize how well you plan your work and reflect on the other side that you are doing a professional job.
-   The most important issue is to determine the boundaries of the project on paper and to agree on paper before analyzing. This step is the primary point you need to do in terms of both the project and your health. Otherwise, neither disagreements nor the project ends. If you skip the stage and enter the project directly, you will have to hear this phrase all the time **“I didn’t want it that way”** and **“Can we add that too?”** …

### Project into Milestones

![](/blog/work-as-a-freelancer/image-5.jpeg)

For example, someone offers you a web project and you set it as $ 40 per hour. You can allocate 5 hours a day and 5 days a week for the project you will receive, and you can reveal this with an average account of how many months you will finish the project. The best way to do this is to divide the project into milestones and plan what challenges we will face and at what stages you will receive financial payments.

When dividing Milestone, you can collect each step under two general headings as Analysis and Design. I would like to give you an example of these stages in a software project using a pattern I use.

Let’s design a milestone, assuming that we will allocate $ 40 per hour and 5 days a week, as mentioned above. With a very simple mathematical formula, we will calculate the total and the fee we will receive per milestone. To take into account the exceptional cases that may arise. Adding to the project completion period for a few weeks in exceptional cases outside the development process.

### Calculation

> **_weeklyWage $1000_** _\= hourlyWage($40) \* Daily(5)hours \* Weekly(5)day;_

> **_monthWage $6000_** _\= weeklyWage \* (unexpectedWeek(2) + (4)weeks);_

> **_Total $24.000_** _\= monthlyWage\* releaseDate4Moth;_

**General Milestone Example**

-   Determination of requirements and analysis (according to the requirements of the project owner) This step helps us to plan everything that will come up during software development. (2 weeks) ($ 2000)
-   Application Architecture Design Architecture (Dashboard, Webpage, API, etc.) (2 weeks) ($ 2000)
-   Database Design (2 weeks) ($ 2000)
-   Frontend Design (Dashboard, Webpage, etc.) (4 weeks) ($ 4000)
-   Starting the Software Development Process (8 weeks) ($ 4000)
-   Project Completion and Release ($ 10000)

I gave a simple example of the milestone above, which can be understood in just 6 steps, and showed you which steps you can charge a piece by piece fee in a freelance project. We have extracted the weekly, monthly and total cost of the project with a simple calculation above. It would be better to distribute this fee for 6 phases according to the time allocated for each milestone step and here it would be better to determine at which step you will charge more. But the larger 40% of the fee. –It would be better to leave 50 of them for project submission or divide them into equal parts for each phase.

**Attention**

Before the project starts, do not forget to tell the project owner these situations that I have mentioned before,

What are these, let’s recall and add some new information.

-   Indicate that the deadline for the completion of the project is an estimated time and may end before and after the relevant milestones. Also, remind that this situation will be shaped according to the user’s wishes.
-   Based on the sample web project above, state that your offer is subject to the development of the project only and that any extra fees will be reflected separately. (Hosting, Domain, etc.)
